Frequently asked questions

Why is the rate on my screen different from the shop’s?

The app shows the shop’s published rate. If our own rate feed pauses for any reason, the app holds the last rate the shop published rather than letting the number drift with the market — so what you see is always a rate we set, not one worked out on the fly. When a rate is not fresh, the app says so on screen.

It says I am offline, but my internet is fine

The app reaches our servers by more than one route, and moves to whichever is answering. If all of them are unreachable from your network, it shows the last rates it saved and marks them clearly rather than showing an empty screen. Once the connection returns, live rates resume on their own.

Can I stop the notifications?

Yes, at any time: iOS Settings → R.R. Jewellers → Notifications. You will still see every rate in the app; you simply will not be told about offers.

The text is too small

Open the menu at the top left. Text Size sits right at the top — S, M, L or XL. The app already picks the largest size your phone can show without cutting anything off.

How do I hold a rate still while I write it down?

Press and hold any rate for a moment. It is captured into a card that does not move, with the time it was taken. Tap anywhere to release it.
